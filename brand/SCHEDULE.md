## Launch day

1. T-2h: "the road opens in 2 hours" + the money GIF.
2. T-0: launch on Pons, copy the token address.
3. T+2min: Railway → remove `PRELAUNCH`, set `TOKEN_ADDRESS`; check `/health`.
4. T+5min: **launch post** (`COPY.md` → hype video, launch-day version) with the live link, then pin it.
5. T+1h: screenshot of the shrine with the first real numbers — "the first sign is close".
6. Reply to every reply for the first two hours. That is the whole growth strategy.

## Rules that keep the account healthy

- One idea per post. The image carries the joke, the text lands it, the last line is always `DALE.`
- Never promise returns. The lore says he brings wealth; the account never says *you will make money*.
- Never claim to be, or to be endorsed by, a real artist or brand.
- Reply in character, short, lowercase, Spanglish. `eyoooo`, `horns up, familia`, `you get chosen`.
- Quote-tweet your own pinned video when the numbers do something interesting; do not delete old posts.
